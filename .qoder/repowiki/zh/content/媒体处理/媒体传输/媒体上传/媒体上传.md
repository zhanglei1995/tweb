### API文件管理器分析
API文件管理器组件负责处理文件上传和下载的核心逻辑，包括分块大小策略、并发控制和错误恢复。

```mermaid
flowchart TD
Start([开始上传]) --> CheckSize["检查文件大小"]
CheckSize --> SizeValid{"文件大小有效?"}
SizeValid --> |否| ReturnError["返回错误"]
SizeValid --> |是| CalculatePartSize["计算分块大小"]
CalculatePartSize --> DeterminePartSize["确定分块大小"]
DeterminePartSize --> InitUpload["初始化上传"]
InitUpload --> UploadLoop["上传循环"]
UploadLoop --> SendPart["发送分块"]
SendPart --> WaitAck["等待确认"]
WaitAck --> AckReceived{"收到确认?"}
AckReceived --> |否| HandleError["处理错误"]
AckReceived --> |是| UpdateProgress["更新进度"]
UpdateProgress --> MoreParts{"还有更多分块?"}
MoreParts --> |是| UploadLoop
MoreParts --> |否| FinalizeUpload["完成上传"]
FinalizeUpload --> ReturnSuccess["返回成功"]
HandleError --> Retry{"可重试?"}
Retry --> |是| SendPart
Retry --> |否| ReturnError
ReturnError --> End([结束])
ReturnSuccess --> End
```
